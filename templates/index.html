How HistoScan Works

A simple three-step workflow powered by Deep Learning

1. Upload Patch

Submit an H&E stained histopathology tissue patch image in JPG or PNG format.

2. Deep Analysis

ResNet-50 CNN analyzes cellular morphology, nuclear patterns, and tissue architecture.

3. Pathology Report

Receive an automated Benign / Malignant classification with a confidence score and stage estimate.

 Why Deep Learning for Histopathology?

Manual analysis of histopathology slides is time-consuming, subjective, and requires highly trained pathologists. Deep Learning — specifically CNNs like ResNet-50 — can learn subtle visual patterns in tissue structure, nuclear morphology, and staining intensity that are invisible to the naked eye. By training on thousands of annotated H&E patches, HistoScan provides a reliable automated second opinion, improving diagnostic throughput and consistency for clinicians worldwide.
